What Makes OptixOS Special?

Engineered with optical domain knowledge that generic retail POS systems lack:

👓

Spectacle Pair Guided Wizard

Prevents costly lab dispensing mistakes through strict optical domain guardrails.

  • 1:1 Lens-Frame Pairing: Exactly one frame pairs with one pair of lenses.
  • Frame Zero-Power Invariant: Frames can never hold diopters accidentally.
  • Sequential Steps: Pick Focus (Single Vision / Progressive) → Pick Material & Index → Pick Anti-Reflective Coatings.
👁️

Clinical Refraction Powers (OD / OS)

Native support for ophthalmic diopters, cylinder axes, and contact lens conversions.

  • 0.25 D Quarter Steps: Standard optical step increments for SPH, CYL, and ADD.
  • Corneal Vertex Converter (BVD): 1-click vertex distance conversion for high powers when fitting contact lenses.
  • Instant Prescription History: Automatically shows the patient's past powers during checkout.
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Account Billing

Indian optical families often shop together under one primary phone number.

  • Single Invoice Checkout: Buy glasses for Father, Mother, and Child on one single bill.
  • Distinct Rx Assignment: Each pair of glasses retains the specific family member's clinical prescription for the lab.
  • Advance Credit Sharing: Share advance deposits across the entire household.
🔬

Optical Workshop Kanban

Real-time status board tracking lenses sent for edging, glazing, and fitting.

  • Drag-and-Drop Stages: Pending Fitting → In Workshop → QC Passed → Ready for Pickup.
  • Financial Redaction: Lab workshop slips omit pricing so technicians only see optical cut details.
  • SLA Overdue Tracking: Automated alerts when a customer order is approaching promised delivery date.
🖨️

Thermal & Laser Print Engine

Compliant with Indian statutory GST rules, HSN codes, and retail hardware printers.

  • 80mm Thermal Receipts: High-speed slips for receipt printers (EPSON, TVS, Star).
  • A4 Laser Tax Invoices: Full 2-column compliance invoices with CGST/SGST itemized breakdown.
  • Digital Receipt Links: Tamper-proof customer WhatsApp sharing links with cryptographic security.
🏢

Multi-Store & Role Security

Run a single dispensary or an eyewear chain across multiple branches.

  • Multi-Store Branch Switcher: Switch views between branches with stock isolation.
  • Wholesale Cost Redaction: Cashiers cannot see wholesale inventory cost prices.
  • Atomic Inventory Locks: Prevents double-selling frames or lenses under high counter traffic.

5-Minute Interactive Test Runbook

Follow these quick steps to test the full billing cycle in live mode:

1

Open the POS Counter

Navigate to http://localhost:3000/pos/new-bill. In the patient search box at top-left, enter phone 9876543210 or search "Rajesh Sharma". Click to select.

2

Add a Spectacle Pair

Press F2 or click "+ Add Product". Click "Spectacle Lenses". Choose your lens options (Single Vision → Blue-Cut Screen Defense) and select a frame (e.g. Ray-Ban Wayfarer). Click "Add to Cart".

3

Verify Prescription & Discount

The cart will automatically attach Rajesh's active clinical prescription. Try typing a discount in ₹ or % in the dual-box discount widget and observe real-time recalculation of GST.

4

Collect Payment & Complete Sale

Press F10 or click "Proceed to Payment". Select payment method (Cash, UPI / QR, Card, or Advance Deposit). Click "Complete & Print Invoice".

5

View in Optical Lab & Financial Reports

Go to Workshop Lab Orders to see the job card in the Kanban queue. Check Day-End Reports to view your live cash drawer and GST totals.
